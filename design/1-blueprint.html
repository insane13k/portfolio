How it's built

The same foundation as Protein Plate, plus a motion layer.

Nothing exotic. Every tool here is free and widely used; the first one is what Protein Plate's website already runs on, so it's proven on your own project.

React + Vite THE FRAME

The structure of the site. Same as Protein Plate's website — fast to build, fast to load.

Tailwind CSS THE LOOK

Styling. Makes the phone and desktop layouts quick to get right and consistent.

Motion THE POLISH

Every fade, slide, reveal and page transition. This is what makes it feel expensive.

Three.js THE 3D

The hero scene, floating shapes, depth on cards. The same engine behind the famous 3D portfolios listed below.

GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis THE SCROLL

Animations tied to how far you've scrolled (the timeline drawing itself), and the smooth-scroll feel.

One content file YOUR PART

Projects, jobs, education and skills live in one plain file. Adding a project later means adding a few lines — no design work.

Why not Wix, Canva or a template? Their free plans stamp their branding on your site, the address is theirs, and none of them can do real 3D. This one is yours, has no branding, and costs nothing.

On phones the design stays identical but the effects get lighter — fewer particles, no custom cursor — so it stays smooth and doesn't drain the battery. It also respects the phone's "reduce motion" setting.
