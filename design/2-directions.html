  • Your name reveals line by line the moment the page opens.

Signature moves
  • A chrome object that breathes and turns toward the cursor — real 3D, drawn to look like polished metal.
  • Text that sharpens out of a blur when the page opens.
  • Full-width project images with a slow, quiet zoom.
  • White space treated as a feature, not a gap.
Choose this if

You want to look expensive and calm. Suits founders, agencies and anyone whose work speaks for itself. Of the four, this one will still look current in five years.

Watch out

Needs discipline — few sections, short words. Less "wow" on a phone, where the object becomes a still sphere. Hardest to fill if you have many small projects.

How to decide.

Open this page on your phone too — every sample is built for it. Then send me one letter, or a mix.

Reply with a letter — or a recipe

Mixing is allowed and often the best answer. Examples that work well together: C's bento hero with A's colour worlds, B's project index inside D's calm layout, or D's chrome object as the hero of C.

If you want my honest pick for "modern, aesthetic, professional": C if you're going after jobs at product companies, B if you're going after clients. D if you want the one people remember quietly. A if you want the one people remember loudly.
